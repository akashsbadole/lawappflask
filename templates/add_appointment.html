Appointment Types

Initial meeting with a potential client to discuss their legal needs and determine if the firm can assist them.

General meeting with clients, colleagues, or other parties to discuss case matters, strategy, or updates.

Court appearance before a judge or magistrate for arguments, motions, or other proceedings.

Alternative dispute resolution process where a neutral third party helps parties reach a settlement.
Scheduling Tips
  • Check the lawyer's availability before scheduling
  • Allow adequate travel time between appointments
  • Consider client preferences for in-person vs. virtual meetings
  • Set reminders for important appointments
  • Include all relevant details in the appointment description
  • For virtual meetings, test the technology in advance
