Time Entry Guidelines
  • Enter detailed descriptions of work performed
  • Always select the appropriate case and client
  • Record time as soon as possible after work is completed
  • Be accurate with start and end times
  • Mark non-billable time appropriately
  • Include relevant details in notes for future reference
  • Review entries for accuracy before submission
