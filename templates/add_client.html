Client Types

Individual clients are private persons seeking legal services for personal matters such as property disputes, family law, criminal defense, etc.

Corporate clients are businesses or organizations seeking legal services for corporate matters such as contracts, compliance, mergers and acquisitions, etc.

Government clients are government agencies or departments seeking legal services for public sector matters such as regulatory compliance, litigation, etc.

NGO clients are non-governmental organizations or non-profits seeking legal services for matters such as incorporation, compliance, advocacy, etc.
Practice Areas

Select the appropriate practice area for the client's legal needs:

  • Property Law - Real estate transactions, property disputes, landlord-tenant issues
  • Corporate Law - Business formation, contracts, mergers and acquisitions
  • Criminal Law - Defense against criminal charges, plea negotiations
  • Family Law - Divorce, child custody, adoption, domestic violence
  • Employment Law - Workplace disputes, discrimination, wrongful termination
  • Intellectual Property - Patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets
  • Tax Law - Tax planning, disputes with tax authorities
  • Immigration Law - Visas, citizenship, deportation defense
  • Environmental Law - Regulatory compliance, pollution cases
  • Other - Any other legal practice area not listed above
Priority Levels

Routine matters with no immediate deadlines. Can be addressed within normal business hours.

Important matters with moderate urgency. Should be addressed within a few days.

Urgent matters requiring immediate attention. Should be addressed within 24 hours.

Critical matters requiring immediate attention. Should be addressed immediately.
