Document Categories

Legal documents filed with the court, such as complaints, answers, motions, and petitions.

Agreements between parties, including service agreements, employment contracts, and settlement agreements.

Letters, emails, and other communications with clients, opposing counsel, courts, and other parties.

Documents, photos, videos, and other materials that support a case, such as exhibits, affidavits, and expert reports.

Orders issued by a judge or court, including judgments, injunctions, and procedural orders.

Legal research materials, including case law summaries, statutory analysis, and memoranda.

Financial documents, including invoices, fee agreements, expense reports, and trust account records.

Administrative documents, including engagement letters, conflict checks, and office policies.
Upload Guidelines
  • Use descriptive filenames that clearly identify the document content
  • Ensure documents are properly scanned and readable
  • Remove any sensitive or confidential information not relevant to the case
  • Organize documents by client and case for easy retrieval
  • Add relevant tags to improve searchability
  • Mark confidential documents appropriately
  • Set expiry dates for time-sensitive documents
  • Comply with data protection and privacy regulations
Document Security
Important: Ensure you have the right to upload and share these documents. Unauthorized sharing of confidential information may violate attorney-client privilege and data protection laws.
  • Confidential documents are only accessible to authorized personnel
  • All document uploads are logged and tracked
  • Documents are encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits are performed on the document storage system
