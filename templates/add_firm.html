Multi-Tenant Information

Multi-tenant support allows multiple law firms to use the same LawEdge system while keeping their data completely separate.

Key Benefits:
  • Data isolation between firms
  • Custom branding per firm
  • Independent user management
  • Separate reporting and analytics
  • Cost-effective solution for multiple firms
Subdomain Setup:

Each firm can have its own subdomain (e.g., yourfirm.lawedge.com) for a branded experience. This is optional but recommended for firms that want a custom URL.

Branding Options:

Each firm can customize its appearance with:

  • Primary and secondary colors
  • Custom logo
  • Branded email templates
  • Firm-specific reports
Tax Information

For legal compliance and billing purposes, each firm should provide their tax identification numbers:

GST Number:

Goods and Services Tax identification number for firms operating in India. Required for GST-compliant invoicing.

PAN Number:

Permanent Account Number issued by the Income Tax Department of India. Required for financial transactions and reporting.
