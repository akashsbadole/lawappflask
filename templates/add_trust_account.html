Trust Account Guidelines
  • Each trust account must be associated with a client
  • Use descriptive account names for easy identification
  • Ensure accurate bank details are provided
  • Set appropriate opening and current balances
  • Mark accounts as interest-bearing if applicable
  • Enable three-way reconciliation for better compliance
  • Clearly define escrow conditions for escrow accounts
  • Regularly reconcile trust accounts with bank statements
Trust Account Types

Accounts holding funds on behalf of individual clients for their legal matters.

Accounts dedicated to specific cases or matters, often with multiple clients.

Accounts holding settlement funds until distribution conditions are met.

Accounts holding client retainer fees to be drawn against as work is performed.

Accounts used for general firm operations that require strict accounting procedures.
Trust Account Statuses

Account is active and can be used for transactions and reconciliations.

Account is temporarily inactive and cannot be used for transactions until reactivated.

Account has been permanently closed and all funds have been transferred or distributed.

Account is pending activation, typically waiting for initial funding or approval.
