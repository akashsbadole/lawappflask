Expense Guidelines
  • Always keep receipts for expenses
  • Record expenses as soon as possible after they occur
  • Assign expenses to the appropriate case or client
  • Mark expenses as billable to client when appropriate
  • Provide detailed descriptions for all expenses
  • Follow firm policies for expense reimbursement
  • Submit expenses promptly for approval
Expense Categories

Airfare, hotel, car rental, mileage, meals, and other travel-related expenses.

Meals with clients, business entertainment, and client development activities.

Stationery, printer ink, paper, and other office supplies and equipment.

Expert witnesses, consultants, court reporters, and other professional services.

Legal research databases, publications, and research-related expenses.

Postage, courier services, and other delivery-related expenses.

Expenses that don't fit into other categories, such as filing fees, subscriptions, etc.
Expense Statuses

Expense is saved but not yet submitted for approval. Can be edited or deleted.

Expense has been submitted for approval and is awaiting review by a supervisor or manager.

Expense has been approved and is ready for reimbursement or billing to client.

Expense has been reimbursed to the person who paid for it.

Expense has been rejected and requires correction or additional information.
