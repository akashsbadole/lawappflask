Transaction Guidelines
  • Select the correct trust account for the transaction
  • Choose the appropriate transaction type
  • Enter accurate amounts and descriptions
  • Include reference numbers for all transactions
  • Mark transactions as requiring approval when necessary
  • Record reconciliation details when available
  • Ensure all transactions comply with trust accounting rules
  • Review all details before submitting
Transaction Types

Funds being added to a trust account, such as client payments, retainer fees, or settlement funds.

Funds being removed from a trust account, such as payments to clients, third parties, or firm fees.

Funds being moved from one trust account to another, such as transferring settlement funds to a distribution account.

Bank fees, transaction fees, or other charges deducted from the trust account.

Interest earned on the trust account balance, which may need to be distributed to clients according to rules.

Corrections or adjustments to previous transactions, such as correcting errors or reconciling discrepancies.
Transaction Statuses

Transaction has been successfully processed and the funds have been moved.

Transaction is awaiting processing, approval, or confirmation from the bank.

Transaction could not be completed due to an error, insufficient funds, or other issues.

Transaction has been reversed, typically due to an error or client request.
