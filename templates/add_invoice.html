Invoice Guidelines
  • Always select the correct client and case
  • Provide clear descriptions for all invoice items
  • Set appropriate due dates based on client agreements
  • Review all calculations before sending
  • Include relevant payment terms
  • Add any special instructions in the notes
  • Consider sending a preview to the client before finalizing
